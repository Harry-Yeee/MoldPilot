# Rollout Conversations Workbook 启动对话手册

Owner: Harry · Created 2026-07-11 · Companion: `docs/06-kpi/kpi-system-design.md` §7 (launch playbook)

Five conversations must happen before launch day. This workbook gives each one a goal, preparation, a step-by-step script, the exact agreements to walk out with, and the traps. Work top to bottom — Conversation 1 gates everything else.

**Rules for every conversation in this book:**
- Bring the phone with the live system, not slides. Show, don't describe.
- Get agreements as specific sentences, repeated back. "基本同意" (basically agree) is not an agreement.
- Write the outcome in the log at the bottom of each section the same day.

---

## Conversation 1 — The CEO session 与CEO的深谈

**Goal:** the CEO signs onto his role in the system — including the parts that constrain HIM — and rehearses the one moment that decides everything.
**When:** before anything is announced. Allow 90 minutes, private, no phones except the demo phone.
**Bring:** the tier-structure and money-machine visuals, the three v2 posters (printed — see docs/07-training/README.md), the live Scores tab with simulated data.

### Part A — Frame it as HIS instrument (10 min)

Open with what he gets, not what he must do:

> "这套系统让你第一次能用事实管理，而不是靠追问和猜测。每周25分钟，你能看到整个厂的真实状态——谁在动，什么卡住了，客户交期危不危险。"
> (This system lets you manage on facts for the first time. 25 minutes a week and you see the whole factory's real state.)

Walk him through the dashboard, the calendar, one leader's scorecard. Let him click.

### Part B — The five commitments, one at a time (20 min)

Do NOT present these as a list to nod at. Read each aloud, ask "can you live with this?", and wait for a yes before moving on:

- [ ] 1. **Same rules for himself.** His approvals, overrides, and response times are logged and visible like everyone else's.
- [ ] 2. **Facts, not moods.** In reviews, only what the board shows gets discussed. No hearsay verdicts.
- [ ] 3. **Praise in public, coach in private.** Red items get help in the meeting, criticism only one-on-one.
- [ ] 4. **Every "what do you need" answered within one week.** (Part C makes this checkable.)
- [ ] 5. **Honest data is never punished.** A true "we missed because I forgot" costs a bonus at most — never a public price. The first person punished for honesty is the last person who tells the truth.

The sentence that usually lands with a boss who is obeyed but not loved:

> "规则也管你，这不是限制你的权力——这是买威信。全厂看到你守自己定的规矩，比任何讲话都有力。"
> (The rules binding you isn't a limit on your power — it's how you buy credibility. The whole factory seeing you keep your own rules beats any speech.)

### Part C — The action tracker (his commitment #4, made real) (10 min)

Propose: every board walk ends with a written list — item / owner / promised date. The list opens the NEXT board walk before anything else. His blockers appear on it with his name.

- [ ] Agreed: board-walk action list exists, reviewed first every week
- [ ] Agreed: who keeps the list (Harry, until the system has a home for it)

### Part D — REHEARSE THE FIRST RED (20 min — the most important 20 minutes in this book)

Set the scene for him explicitly:

> "第三周的周五，屏幕上会出现一个红色超时，旁边是某位老师傅的名字。全屋的人都会看你的脸。你说的第一句话，决定这套系统是活是死。"
> (Week three, Friday, a red overdue appears next to a veteran's name. The whole room watches your face. Your first sentence decides whether this system lives or dies.)

Then actually rehearse. You play the room. He practices saying, verbatim:

> **"这个卡在哪里？需要我做什么？" (Where is this stuck? What do you need from me?)**

Run it three times with different scenarios: a machine conflict, a lazy excuse, a genuine mistake. The lazy-excuse round matters most — the scripted response is still the same sentence, followed by silence. The board and the month-end bar deliver the consequence; his job in the room is only to unblock. If he cannot resist adding a lecture, note it and rehearse again another day. Do not launch until this is clean.

- [ ] Rehearsed 3 scenarios; he can hold the script

### Part E — Prize governance + go-criteria + Plan B (20 min)

- [ ] **Veto rule:** winners are decided mechanically by the data; he may veto ONLY with a written, logged reason. Get the yes explicitly.
- [ ] **Pilot go-criteria agreed IN NUMBERS** (fill in together):
  - ≥ ____% of trials recorded in-system during month 1 (suggest 80%)
  - ≥ ____% of date confirmations happening in-app (suggest 70%)
  - ≤ ____ data disputes escalated (suggest 3)
  - If met → prize months start. If not → one more calibration month, rules adjusted, no blame.
- [ ] **Plan B agreed:** if resistance is severe, the retreat is "records only, scores paused for a quarter" — never full abandonment, never announced as failure. He agrees to this in advance so month-two panic can't kill the system.
- [ ] **Baseline month amnesty:** he will personally say the amnesty sentence at the announcement (Conversation 5).

## Conversation 2 — HR / labor & finance 人事与财务

**Goal:** the money is legally clean and operationally boring.
**Who:** whoever handles labor contracts + whoever handles cash/payroll.
**When:** after the CEO session, before any announcement.

### Steps

1. Describe the scheme in one sentence: a **discretionary monthly bonus pool (酌情奖金)** of ¥5,000 tied to system participation — not wages, not a deduction from anything existing.
2. Ask the two legal questions (write the answers down):
   - [ ] Does this need an employee-handbook clause or contract addendum to be safely discretionary? (员工手册需要加条款吗？)
   - [ ] Any constraint on displaying individual performance data internally? (Tiered visibility: own score + department boards public, individual rankings management-only.)
3. Draft the one paragraph with them: bonus is discretionary, criteria published monthly, no entitlement accrues, management reserves adjustment with notice.
   - [ ] Paragraph drafted and filed
4. Payday logistics — decide precisely:
   - [ ] Payment day (suggest: within 3 days of the monthly meeting)
   - [ ] Form: cash in red envelopes 红包 handed at the meeting (ceremony value) vs payroll transfer (clean records) — or both: announce at meeting, pay via payroll same week
   - [ ] Tax treatment confirmed by finance
   - [ ] Who physically prepares it
5. Say this sentence to finance and mean it: **"The first payment being exact and on time matters more than any feature I have built."**

## Conversation 3 — Leader one-on-ones 组长逐一沟通 (×6)

**Goal:** each award-tier leader sees THEIR OWN numbers privately before anyone else does, and their objections improve the rules before launch.
**Who:** Design leader, PM ×2, PM assistant, Assembly leaders ×2, Injection leader. 30 minutes each, at their workstation not an office.
**When:** during the baseline month, after 2+ weeks of data exists.

### Script per leader

1. Open the phone. Show THEIR task list first — "这就是你每天要看的，就这一屏。" (This is all you look at daily — one screen.)
2. Show their own scorecard. Walk one line item down to the raw record: "每一分都能点开查证，没有暗箱。" (Every point opens to the record. No black box.)
3. Say the two protections out loud, personally: honest reasons never punished; fewer than 5 events counts as passing.
4. Explain their ¥400 in their own numbers: "上个月你这个数字是 X%。达到85%，400块是你的，跟别人无关——帮别的组长永远不吃亏。"
5. **Then shut up and listen.** Ask: "哪条规则你觉得不合理？" (Which rule feels unfair to you?) Write every objection down in front of them. Do not defend the system in this meeting.
   - Objection raised: __________________ → rule change considered? Y/N
6. Close with the ask: "第一个月是校准月，不发钱不算账。我需要你把数据录全——不是为了考核，是为了让规则先被你们检验。"
   (Month one is calibration. I need complete data — not to judge you, but so YOU can test the rules first.)

- [ ] Lin (Design) · [ ] Bill (PM) · [ ] Jun/Cheng (PM) · [ ] PM assistant · [ ] Zhong (Assembly A) · [ ] Pei (Assembly B) · [ ] Wang (Injection)

7. **The week-1 claiming pact 第一周认领之约:** agree that this leader personally claims the first issues in his inbox during launch week — presses 我来处理 in front of the crew. Nobody volunteers until they watch a leader claim and survive. The first crab-eater is a leader, by arrangement, not by luck.

**Trap:** do not promise rule changes on the spot. "我记下了，周五会上定" (noted, we decide Friday) — rules change in the ritual, not in corridors, or the ritual has no authority.

## Conversation 4 — The referees briefing 裁判简报 (QC leader + Marketing leader, together)

**Goal:** they understand referee status as an honor with pay, not an exclusion — and they understand why their neutrality is the system's foundation.
**When:** after leader one-on-ones, before the announcement. 30 minutes, both together (it defines them as a pair).

### Script

1. Open with the frame: "整套系统的分数都建立在你们的结论上。所以你们不参赛——裁判不能被收买，这是对你们的信任，也是保护。"
   (Every score in this system is built on your verdicts. That's why you're not in the competition — a referee can't be bought. This is trust, and protection.)
2. Show their service bars: QC — report ≤2 days, verdict at the verifying trial; Marketing — date decision ≤1 day. **State clearly what is NOT measured: which way they decide.** Pass rates and approval rates are facts, never targets.
3. The allowance: ¥250/month each when the service bar is met. Paid for tempo and completeness, never for verdicts.
4. Marketing's extra card: double votes on Friday — "你们是客户在厂里的声音" (you are the customer's voice in this factory).
5. Ask each: "什么情况下你会觉得有人在给你施压改结论？" (When would you feel pressured to change a verdict?) — write it down; these are the audit triggers you'll quietly watch.

- [ ] QC leader briefed · [ ] Marketing leader briefed
- [ ] Both can repeat back: measured on speed, never on direction

## Conversation 5 — The announcement meeting 全员宣布会

**Goal:** the whole company hears the same story once, from the CEO's mouth, with the amnesty and the fixer-principle stated as law.
**When:** after 1–4 are complete and the baseline month has begun quietly. 30 minutes, everyone, posters on the wall behind the screen.

### Agenda (run it exactly)

1. **CEO opens (5 min) — he must personally say, in this order:**
   - The amnesty: "系统启用之前的所有旧账，一笔勾销。第一个月只校准，不考核，不追责。"
   - The principle: "负责人是解决问题的人，不是犯错的人。"
   - The self-binding: "这套规则同样管我。我的审批、我的否决，全都记录在案，你们都能看到。"
   - The protection: "如实填写，永不追责。"
2. **Harry demos (10 min):** one issue's journey on the big screen — file with photo → Friday vote → claim → fix → verified → library. Then one leader's task list on a phone. Nothing else. Resist showing every feature.
3. **The money (5 min):** the scores-and-money poster (§5, the four pools). State the date of the first prize meeting.
   - Every prize meeting: print the monthly snapshot, read its integrity code (校验码) aloud, CEO + both referees sign the page — file the signed page in the binder.
4. **Questions (10 min):** the CEO answers culture questions; Harry answers system questions. Planted first question if the room is silent: have a friendly leader ask "扣不扣工资？" so the "bonus only, never a deduction" answer gets said loudly.
5. **Close:** install day announcement — when and where everyone gets the app on their phone.
