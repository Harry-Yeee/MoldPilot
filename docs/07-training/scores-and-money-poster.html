① 达标线The bar — a habit, not perfection

85%
按时完成率 ≥ 85% = 达标 → ¥400
  • 15 件事做好 13 件就够——不要求完美，要求习惯13 of 15 on time is enough — habit, not perfection
  • 当月不足 5 件事：直接视为达标——安静的月份不吃亏Fewer than 5 events = automatic pass. Quiet months never hurt you
  • 未达标：没有处罚，只是拿不到当月达标奖。工资永远不扣Missing the bar costs nothing — the bonus just isn't earned. Wages are never touched
  • 组长的达标 = 全组数据完整。组员做了但没录 = 没做A leader passes when the whole group's data is complete
